# Trademarks

CADD Calculator's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE.md). Its
name and logo are protected differently, so that people can always tell the official app
from a modified one. This page says what you can do with them.

## The marks

* the name **"CADD Calculator"**
* the **symbol**: the drafting set-square forming a "C"
* the **logo**: the symbol with the name
* the **app icon**: the symbol on a crimson tile

They're the files in `src/caddcalc/gui/assets/brand/` and `docs/dev/design/brand/`,
described in the [brand guide](docs/dev/design/brand.md). The marks belong to the
project's maintainer, [inmymoonsuit](https://github.com/inmymoonsuit).

The files themselves are under Apache-2.0, like the rest of the repo. That licence covers
copying and changing them; it grants no right to use the marks to identify a product (its
section 6). That use is what this page governs.

## You can, without asking

* **Refer to the project** by its name: write about it, review it, teach with it, and link
  to it.
* **Redistribute official builds and the source, unmodified,** with the name and the logo
  as they are.
* **Say your work is "based on" or "compatible with" CADD Calculator,** when it is.

## A modified version needs its own name and icon

A fork, or any build with changed code, formulas, or data, must use **a different name,
icon, and logo**, so its users can tell it isn't the official app. It must not suggest
that this project endorses it. The fork may keep the brand files in its history and its
repo, but its builds mustn't present themselves with them. A branding pack is how a build
supplies its own icon and logo ([packaging](docs/dev/packaging.md#branding-packs)). The
name is set in the code.

## You can't

* use the marks for an unrelated product or service
* use them in a way that suggests this project endorses you, your product, or your work

Ask first: open an issue at
[github.com/inmymoonsuit/CADD-Calculator/issues](https://github.com/inmymoonsuit/CADD-Calculator/issues).
